# Website specifications

These specifications translate the competitor observations into testable ideas for the T-shirt website. They are proposed design work, not claims that a store or feature is already implemented.

| Research basis | Proposed decision | Specification | Validation check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CheapesTees and Jiffy show scannable product grids (`E06`, `E10`). Crazy Dog's floating prompt competes for attention (`E01`). | Use a restrained layout with no spin-the-wheel overlay. | Product cards show an image, title, fabric weight, base price, and rating. Use up to four columns on desktop and two on mobile. | Ask a peer to find the price and fabric weight within five seconds. |
| Jiffy displays size/color pricing (`E11`); Crazy Dog's captured product page does not show fabric weight above the fold (`E03`). | Put useful garment and price details beside size selection. | Show size options with each size's price, color-specific price differences, fabric composition, and a fit guide before “Add to cart.” | Ask a peer to select a size and state the price before adding the item. |
| Competitor pages show aggregate review counts; the audit does not establish fit/shrinkage answers (`E02`, `E06`, `E10`). | Organize wearer feedback around the questions shoppers need answered. | Where genuine reviews exist, summarize fit, fabric feel/weight, and after-wash print quality. Label reviewer height and size only when provided. | Ask five peers whether they can describe the fit without opening a separate size chart. |
| Crazy Dog defers shipping (`E04`); CheapesTees shows a preselected `$0.49` add-on (`E08`); Jiffy shows delivery choices (`E12`). | Make total-cost components visible and optional extras opt-in. | Show a ZIP-code shipping estimate, free-shipping threshold, and discount terms in the cart. Never preselect protection or other paid add-ons. | Ask five peers to identify the delivered total from the cart without continuing to payment. |

## Proposed design tradeoffs

- A calm grid may feel less immediately entertaining than a promo-heavy landing page.
- Attribute-specific reviews need real, collected customer feedback; do not fabricate ratings or reviewer details.
- A shipping estimator must not imply a guaranteed delivery date or price unless the store can actually honor it.

## Validation plan

Before treating the design choices as proven, run a small peer walkthrough from homepage to cart. Record task completion time and whether participants can state the selected shirt's fit information, item price, and estimated delivery cost. Revise the specifications based on what they struggle to find.
