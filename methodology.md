# Research method and limitations

## Scope

- **Market and device:** US storefronts, inspected in a desktop Chrome browser.
- **Audit dates:** November 4–5, 2026; the 12 embedded screenshots are dated November 5.
- **Shared scenario:** A first-time shopper near Butler, NJ (`07405`) selects one short-sleeve adult crewneck in size Small or Medium, reviews product pricing, and advances to cart/checkout entry.
- **Competitors:** Crazy Dog T-Shirts (novelty graphics), CheapesTees (wholesale blanks and custom basics), and Jiffy (blank apparel and custom printing).
- **Stages:** Homepage, category/listing, product pricing/detail, and cart/checkout entry for each competitor.

The products and purchase configurations differ across merchants. They are useful for comparing interface and buying-flow decisions, but their prices are not a controlled, like-for-like comparison.

## Evidence practice

The `E01–E12` register and screenshot captions are the factual basis for the comparison. The audit did not create accounts, subscribe to mailing lists, or submit personal payment information. Merchant claims such as “Most Loved,” savings, ratings, and delivery promises are recorded as displayed, not treated as independently verified.

The analysis applies three interpretive lenses:

- **Brand archetypes:** Margaret Mark and Carol S. Pearson's archetype framework.
- **Persuasion principles:** Robert Cialdini's influence principles, with price anchoring and risk reduction kept distinct.
- **Design reading:** Modernist and postmodernist traits using the Victoria and Albert Museum's overviews.

These lenses support interpretation; they do not prove a merchant's intent or customer response.

## Limits and unresolved details

- Final shipping and taxes were not confirmed for Crazy Dog or CheapesTees because checkout address entry was not completed.
- The CheapesTees cart includes a preselected `$0.49` Shipping Protection option; the final shipping rate still requires a ZIP lookup.
- Jiffy's custom-print cart is not directly comparable to a blank-shirt price. The `$48.33` total includes a custom item and selected rush shipping.
- Review authenticity was not independently audited.
- The report's text and embedded screenshots differ on the CheapesTees comparison price and some Jiffy details. See [competitor comparison](./competitors.md) and [evidence captions](./screenshots.md).
- The captures record a point in time. Prices, offers, ratings, stock, and delivery dates may have changed.
- The two personas are hypotheses. No customer interviews are claimed.
- The proposed design choices need peer usability validation; no conversion impact is established.
